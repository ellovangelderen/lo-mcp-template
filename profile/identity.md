# Identity

Who you are, in one page. This is the section an agent reads first — and the
only one it reads if it can read only one. Keep it current and honest.

Cover:
- Name, where you live, what you do for a living.
- The two or three things that actually drive you.
- What "a good outcome" looks like for you in daily work.

Example line:
> Alex Doe, Utrecht. Runs a small consultancy; values working results over
> plans, and short mornings over long meetings.
