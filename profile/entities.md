# Entities

The separate tracks of your life (employer, own company, board roles, family)
and the walls between them. Agents use this to keep contexts apart.

Cover, per track:
- Name, what it is, your role.
- What may and may not mix with other tracks (e.g. employer data never leaves
  employer systems).

Example line:
> Work (employer X): confidential; never store or process its content in my
> personal systems.
